T(n) = 4 * T(n/2) + 1
     = 4 * (4 * T(n/4) + 1) + 1
     = 4 * (4 * (4 * T(n/8) + 1) + 1) + 1
     = 4^k * T(n / 2 ^ k ) + 4^(k-1) + 4^(k-2) + ... + 4^0

n = 2^k => k = log2n
